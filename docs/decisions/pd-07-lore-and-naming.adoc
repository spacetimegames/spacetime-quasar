= PD-07: Lore and naming: agent-proposed, owner-validated
:description: Product decision PD-07

* *Date:* 2026-10-10
* *Resolves:* open point #8 (review ref G15)

*Decision.* Agents draft all lore with dev-time LLMs (xref:pd-05-llm-usage.adoc[PD-05]). The product owner
validates every piece before it is committed as data.

*Order of work:*

. Main storylines.
. Civilisation and species types (feeding the per-faction ship design grammars of xref:pd-04-multiplayer-is-not-supported.adoc[PD-04]).
. Factions, systems, planets and stations.
. Ships, commodities and mission texts.

*Constraints.* All content is original: no names, lore, ships or assets from
EV: Nova or other IP. Lore is written in English.

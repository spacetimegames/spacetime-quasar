= PD-10: VR is not supported in the MVP, but the architecture is VR-ready
:description: Product decision PD-10

* *Date:* 2026-10-10
* *Amends:* the agent charter (private) §1 (VR listed as out of scope)

*Context.* Space combat is one of VR's strongest genres (_Elite Dangerous VR_,
_Star Wars: Squadrons_ VR, _EVE: Valkyrie_). VR is costly for several reasons:

* ~9 M pixels per frame at a stable 90 fps (11.1 ms), against ~2 M at 60 fps for 1080p;
* several cinematic post effects cause discomfort;
* it needs a cockpit view, and therefore cockpit interiors;
* wgpu has no built-in XR, so it goes through OpenXR via the `openxr` crate and Vulkan interop.

*Decision.* VR is out of scope for the MVP. VR becomes a post-MVP goal together
with free 6-DOF flight (xref:pd-01-flight-model.adoc[PD-01] option B) and a procedural cockpit. Target VR
platforms are Windows and Linux. The architecture stays VR-ready through these
binding constraints:

. *The camera abstraction supports N views.* The renderer must not assume a single view (stereo via wgpu `MULTIVIEW` later).
. *The HUD can render in world space* (diegetic), not only in screen space.
. *Every post effect can be toggled individually* per render profile, so a future VR profile can disable motion blur, chromatic aberration, film grain and problematic screen-space effects.
. *The cockpit is a section in the xref:pd-03-ship-progression.adoc[PD-03] ship model*, so a procedural interior can be added later without changing the design format.

*Notes.* The product owner has no headset. VR validation hardware is a future
procurement decision. On Linux the options are Monado, WiVRn (Quest streaming) and
SteamVR; macOS has no practical VR path.

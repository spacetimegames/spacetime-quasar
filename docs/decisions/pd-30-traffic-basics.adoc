= PD-30: Traffic basics: four archetypes, medium density
:description: Product decision PD-30

* *Date:* 2026-10-11
* *Resolves:* design backlog G9, basic part (SPAC-112)

*Context.* Phase 2 needs AI archetypes and traffic density so systems feel inhabited.

*Decision.*

* The MVP archetypes are trader, pirate, patrol and escort.
* Density is medium, with a per-system "busy" setting.
* As placeholders, a quiet system has about 5–10 ships in range and a hub about 20–40. 200 ships is only the stress budget.
* Ships visibly arrive and leave through the jump link.
* Pirate frequency varies by system.
* A richer role + faction + temperament model was considered and deferred. Archetypes stay data-driven so it can be added.

*Alternatives considered.*

* Sparse scripted traffic (_EV: Nova_): cheap, but systems feel empty.
* Dense ambient traffic (_X4_, _Freelancer_): lively, but costly and harder to tune.

*Consequences.*

* Archetypes and per-system settings live in data files.
* The numbers above are placeholders to tune during Phase 2.

*Reference games.* _EV: Nova_, _X4_, _Freelancer_.

= PD-02: Landing style and spaceport contents (vertical slice)
:description: Product decision PD-02

* *Date:* 2026-10-10
* *Resolves:* open point #2 (review ref G3)

*Context.* In EV: Nova, landing is an instant cut to a menu-driven spaceport. The
prompt listed 3 ship classes and a "starter" shuttle, but no way to obtain the others.

*Decision.*

. *Landing / docking is an automatic cinematic.* The player requests landing; the ship docks by itself with a staged camera shot (2–5 s, skippable); then the spaceport menu opens. There is no manually flown docking.
. *Spaceport screens in the vertical slice:*
** *Commodity exchange.*
** *Mission board.*
** *Services:* refuel and repair.
** *Shipyard:* buy the other ship classes, with trade-in.
** *Outfitter, minimal (5–6 items):* missile ammo, countermeasures, an upgraded laser, a cargo expansion, a shield upgrade.
. *Bar is out of the slice.* Escort hiring, if any, goes through the mission board.

*Alternatives considered.*

* Instant menu cut (EV: Nova, Starsector) wastes the 3D presentation.
* Manually flown docking (Elite Dangerous without a docking computer) is slow and repetitive, and awkward with planar flight (xref:pd-01-flight-model.adoc[PD-01]).
* A full outfitter is deferred: it needs a hardpoint/slot system, per-item data and visible mounted equipment. See open point #10 for the ship-progression model it must fit into.

*Consequences.*

* The docking cinematic doubles as a showcase shot for procedural stations and ships.
* Shipyard and outfitter items are data (`assets/**/*.ron`), not code.

*Reference games.*

* _Rebel Galaxy_, _Freelancer_, _Elite Dangerous_ (docking computer) — cinematic docking.
* _EV: Nova_, _Starsector_ — menu spaceports.

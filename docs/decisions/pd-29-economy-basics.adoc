= PD-29: Economy basics: light supply and demand
:description: Product decision PD-29

* *Date:* 2026-10-11
* *Resolves:* design backlog G5, basic part (SPAC-111)

*Context.* Phase 2 needs a basic economy so trading and early progression can be built. The full economy is a Phase 3 item.

*Decision.* Light supply and demand.

* A port's starting prices come from its economy type, shift with the player's trades, and recover over a few game hours.
* There are no production chains.
* 1 ton = 1 unit for every commodity.
* The player starts poor, in the shuttle, with one laser.
* There are 10–12 placeholder commodities, including 2 illegal ones. Final names come from the PD-07 naming work.
* Patrols scan. Being caught means a fine and lower standing with that faction. Detailed law rules come with G6.
* NPC trade is decoration only for now and does not move prices.

*Alternatives considered.*

* Fixed prices (_EV: Nova_): simplest, but trading has no feedback.
* Production chains (_X4_): deepest, but too large for Phase 2.

*Consequences.*

* Price data and economy types live in data files.
* The full economy is decided before Phase 3.

*Reference games.* _EV: Nova_, _X4_.

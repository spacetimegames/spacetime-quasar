= PD-12: Visual style method
:description: Product decision PD-12

* *Date:* 2026-10-10
* *Resolves:* open point #9

*Decision.*

* *3D look:* a written art bible in `docs/` covering the material palette (alloys, paints, roughness ranges), per-system lighting rules, and real photographic references collected by the product owner. It is validated through renderer screenshots and the beauty corner (xref:pd-08-development-order.adoc[PD-08]).
* *HUD/UI:* during the art pass (xref:pd-08-development-order.adoc[PD-08]), 2–3 style directions are explored with a Design artifact. The product owner picks one, which is converted into design tokens (colour, type, spacing) that `st-hud` implements procedurally. Until then the UI is functional greybox.

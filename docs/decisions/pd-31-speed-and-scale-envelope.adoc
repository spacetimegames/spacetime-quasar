= PD-31: Speed and scale envelope (provisional)
:description: Product decision PD-31

* *Date:* 2026-10-11
* *Resolves:* the speed-envelope question listed in xref:../adr/0007-large-world-coordinates.adoc[ADR 0007]
* *Status:* provisional, to be tuned by playtesting

*Context.* ADR 0007 assumes normal flight at most 1,000 m/s, cruise at most 10 times that, systems about 2,000 km in radius and bodies 1 to 2 minutes apart at normal speed. The owner could not picture these numbers in the abstract.

*Decision.*

* Use the proposed numbers as starting values only: "let's try them".
* They are not final. They become final only when the owner confirms them after a hands-on test.
* All of them live in data (`assets/**/*.ron`) and are easy to tune.
* The Phase 1 and Phase 2 hands-on demos must let the owner feel the speeds and distances and adjust them.

*Alternatives considered.*

* Fixing the numbers now: rejected, the owner cannot judge them without flying.

*Consequences.*

* No code constant depends on these values. Anything derived from them (validity bound, precision margins) is checked against the data when it changes.
* ADR 0007 keeps its engineering assumptions until the owner confirms the values.

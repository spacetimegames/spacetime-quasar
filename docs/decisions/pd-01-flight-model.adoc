= PD-01: Flight model: planar by default, 3D-ready simulation
:description: Product decision PD-01

* *Date:* 2026-10-10
* *Resolves:* open point #1 (review refs G1, C11)

*Context.* EV: Nova is a planar game: rotate, thrust, inertia, speed cap. The agent charter (private)
D1 says "full 6-DOF 3D presentation", but the design never said whether _gameplay_
is planar or full 3D. That choice drives controls, camera, AI, combat feel and scope.

*Decision.*

. *Default flight is planar gameplay with full 3D presentation* (option C).
** Ships move on a plane with EV-style controls.
** The chase camera sits behind and above the ship.
** Banking on turns and varied altitude of celestial bodies are visual only.
. *An in-plane combat mode ships in the vertical slice.* A toggle adds:
** mouse aim;
** lateral thrust (strafe);
** decoupled facing vs. velocity (flight-assist-off style);
** a closer, lower camera.
+
The ship never leaves the plane. AI stays planar.

. *The simulation is 3D-native with a releasable planar constraint.* Ship state is full 3D: position, orientation quaternion, linear and angular velocity. The plane is enforced as a constraint (e.g. Rapier `LockedAxes`) rather than by a 2D model.
. *Free 6-DOF flight (option B) is a post-MVP extension.* When added, it requires:
** 3D-capable AI so NPCs can follow the player off the plane;
** a controlled return-to-plane transition when leaving B.
+
It must not require rewriting the simulation.

*Alternatives considered.*

* (A) Pure top-down planar. Most EV-faithful, but wastes the photoreal PBR target because ships are seen only from above.
* (B) Full 6-DOF only. Immersive, but brings hard controls, disorientation, an order-of-magnitude harder AI, and a shift of genre toward dogfight sim.
* (B+C) Switchable at any time from day one. If NPCs stay planar, players exploit altitude; if NPCs follow in 3D, the project pays nearly the full cost of B.

*Consequences.*

* The agent charter (private) D1 is reworded: "full 3D presentation, planar gameplay by default".
* `st-sim` stores 3D state. AI, collision queries and the determinism test run under the planar constraint in the MVP.
* The combat mode is mostly input and camera work (`st-game`, input mapping); it needs no extra physics.
* Design note: if B later dominates combat, C becomes a travel and escape mode. Balance this deliberately when B lands.

*Reference games.*

* _Escape Velocity: Nova_ — planar baseline.
* _Rebel Galaxy_ — planar gameplay, 3D presentation.
* _Elite Dangerous_ — flight-assist toggle, 6-DOF.
* _Star Wars: Squadrons_, _Everspace_ — full 6-DOF.

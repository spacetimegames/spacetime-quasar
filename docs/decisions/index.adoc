= Product decisions
:description: Index of the product decisions (PD-xx) recorded during the design phase.

Product decisions (PD-xx) are taken by the product owner while resolving the xref:open-points.adoc[open points].
Each entry is final unless superseded by a later entry.
They are decisions of record for the agent charter (private) and, where architectural, are refined into ADRs under `docs/adr/`.

The `PD-` prefix avoids clashing with the charter's own decision ids (`D1` ... `D11`).

Supporting material: xref:research-gpu-relative-performance-2026-10.adoc[GPU relative performance research (2026-10)].

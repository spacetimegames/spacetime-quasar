= PD-21: Scope cut: cinematic post effects deferred past the MVP
:description: Product decision PD-21

* *Date:* 2026-10-11
* *Resolves:* review item S (partially)

*Decision.* Motion blur, film grain and chromatic aberration are *not in the MVP*.
The MVP post stack keeps bloom, ACES tonemapping, vignette, TAA/FXAA and sharpening.
The deferred effects must still fit the "every post effect individually toggleable"
architecture (xref:pd-10-vr-post-mvp.adoc[PD-10]).

*Rationale.* These are among the effects players disable most often, and VR must
disable them anyway.

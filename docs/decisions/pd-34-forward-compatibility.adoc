= PD-34: Forward compatibility of saves and designs
:description: Product decision PD-34

* *Date:* 2026-10-11
* *Resolves:* the compatibility question of xref:../adr/0010-data-schema-versioning.adoc[ADR 0010]

*Context.* ADR 0010 refuses, with a clear error, to open a save or design written by a newer build. The question was whether to keep that.

*Decision.*

* Keep the ADR 0010 policy. An older build refuses to open a save or design written by a newer build, with a clear error.
* Newer builds keep opening older files.

*Alternatives considered.*

* Lenient reading of newer files by older builds: rejected, it can silently drop content on re-save.

*Consequences.*

* No change to ADR 0010.
* Still open and not decided here: whether a design that fails the current rules can be opened in the shipyard for repair.
